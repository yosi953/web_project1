Welcome to Anfield

Liverpool Football Club is a professional football club based in Liverpool, England. Founded in 1892, the club competes in the Premier League and plays its home matches at the historic Anfield stadium.

Major Club Honours

Liverpool is one of the most successful football clubs in English and European history:

  • 19 First Division / Premier League Titles
  • 6 European Cups / UEFA Champions League Trophies
  • 8 FA Cups
  • 10 League Cups
  • 3 UEFA Cups
