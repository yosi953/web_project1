Iconic Managers

Three legendary managers shaped the identity and success of the club:

  1. Bill Shankly (1959–1974) – Built the modern foundation of the club.
  2. Bob Paisley (1974–1983) – Won three European Cups in nine seasons.
  3. Jürgen Klopp (2015–2024) – Restored domestic and European glory.
